Privacy Policy

Journey Elite (app ID com.qrbase.journeyelite) is an independent game made and published by Công Phương, an individual developer based in Vietnam. In this page, “I” means the developer and “you” means the player.

Data I collect

The game has no account and never asks for your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. I do not receive any data that identifies you. The game does include two Google services, described below, which receive device data so that the game can show optional video ads and so that I can see how the game is played as a whole.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, furniture, staff, your restaurant's name, sound and language settings) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to me or anyone else.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Now and then a passer-by in the game offers coins for watching a short video ad. An ad only plays when you tap that offer, and you can close it. Nothing else in the game shows ads, and there are no in-app purchases.
  • Every ad request is marked for child-directed treatment and limited to content rated suitable for general audiences. Ads are never personalised for anyone.
  • To deliver ads, Google AdMob receives your IP address (and the approximate location it implies), device and operating system details, the app's identifiers, and how you interact with the ad. AdMob may use device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android, for limited purposes like frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement, not to build an advertising profile. The game does not ask iOS for tracking permission.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

  • The game sends anonymous usage events, such as the level you reach, which menus you open and whether a video ad finished, together with an app instance ID, device and operating system details, country and app version.
  • The advertising ID is not collected for analytics, and ad personalisation signals are switched off.
  • I only see aggregated reports. I use them to balance and improve the game, never to profile individual players.

Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads or analytics use your device data. Analytics stays off unless you agree to storing and accessing information on your device.
  • In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information through the same message.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options, shown in the game wherever the law requires it.
  • Outside those regions, ads and analytics run with the settings above, still without personalisation.

Sharing, transfers and retention

  • I do not sell personal data. Data goes to Google as my service provider, and Google may process it in the United States and other countries. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Analytics event data is kept for no longer than 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • The game otherwise works offline. The “Read the full version” button only opens this page in your browser. Google Play and the App Store may also collect data under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, Brazil's LGPD and similar laws), you can ask to access or delete data linked to your device, withdraw consent, object to processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. Withdrawing consent is done in Settings → Privacy options. For any other request, email me. Because I cannot see who you are, I may ask for details from your device to find the right data. Uninstalling the app deletes all game data on your device.

Children

  • Because younger players may still find it, every player is treated as a child for ads and data: ads are child-directed and never personalised, ad content is limited to general audiences, analytics never uses the advertising ID, and no profile is built about anyone.
  • Parents and guardians can contact me to ask for data linked to a child's device to be deleted, or uninstall the app at any time. I encourage them to help young players keep a healthy play schedule.

Age rating and health

  • The game is for players aged 13 and over: a cartoon simulation with no weapon-based combat and no horror, violent or sexual imagery or sound. The official age rating is the one shown by each app store (IARC on Google Play, Apple's rating on the App Store).
